FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need prior Wikimedia experience?

No. These programs are designed to make open-source and Wikimedia contribution approachable for students at different experience levels.

What can I learn through these programs?

You can explore Wikimedia technologies, open-source collaboration, contribution workflows, community practices, and practical software-development skills.

How do I learn more or get involved?

Open the details page for the program that interests you, or contact the WikiClub Tech team at wikitech@indicwiki.org for guidance.
